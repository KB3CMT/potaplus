POTA.APP Customization

pota.app now has its own Band, Mode, Program, QRT, Age, Hidden, Need/ATNO and hunted filters, a per-spot Hide button, NEED/ATNO pills, N1CC and Op-to-Op chips, RBN/PSKReporter links and a column picker (hide Spotter/Comments). POTAPlus keeps what the site does not do: QSY, logging, Spot+Log, Region filter, WAS/Locations badges, SFI/A/K, clock and award widgets.
